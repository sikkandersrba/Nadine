Creating Experiences Since 2016

Wideview Innovation was established in Erode, Tamil Nadu, in 2016 with a vision to create meaningful experiences through innovative event management and community engagement. The foundation was laid by Mrs. Vijayalakshmi Krishnamoorthy, whose son Mr. Dinesh Kumar became the driving force behind the company's success.

Our journey began with a prestigious collaboration with RTV and Bharani Silks, organizing a large-scale Drawing Competition that received tremendous public appreciation. This marked the beginning of a remarkable journey in Tamil Nadu's event management industry.

Following this success, we partnered with RCN TV and Bava TV for numerous cultural programs, talent shows, and public engagement events — building our reputation as a reliable and creative event management partner across Tamil Nadu.

Legacy

LANDMARK ACHIEVEMENTS

Milestones that shaped Wideview Innovation's identity and proved our capability to deliver world-class events across South India.

🎬

South Indian Short Film Festival & koneX Awards

Organized for 5 consecutive years, this festival brought together talented short film creators from across South India, providing hundreds of aspiring filmmakers with professional recognition and an award platform comparable to major entertainment ceremonies. Supported by RTV, RCN TV, and Bava TV.

🏠

Legends of Dance (LOD) — Texvalley

One of South India's largest dance competitions conducted at Texvalley, Erode, attracting over 8,000 participants from different regions. The overwhelming response established Wideview as a trusted name for large-scale cultural productions in Tamil Nadu.

🏢

Caterpillar MOU Event at SIPCOT Erode

Flawless execution of a major corporate event for Caterpillar during its MOU signing at SIPCOT, Erode — appreciated globally for its professional planning, precision management, and operational excellence that set a new benchmark for corporate events in the region.

🏆

Talents of Tamil Nadu 2023

A grand talent and fashion platform providing aspiring artists, models, performers, and young talents a professional opportunity to showcase their abilities. A landmark success that further strengthened Wideview's position in Tamil Nadu's entertainment and talent ecosystem.

🎨

Artistry Cultural Festival

Conceptualized and organized over multiple editions, Artistry celebrated music, dance, art, creativity, and youth talent. The festival became a major success among participants and audiences, establishing it as a beloved cultural event in Tamil Nadu.

🎚

Texvalley Preferred Event Partner

Became Texvalley's preferred event partner, successfully managing fashion shows, corporate events, product launches, business conferences, entertainment programs, and year-long Tata Gold Plus promotional campaigns through Texvalley's franchise operations.

Social Impact

Jeeviyam & Community Welfare

Social responsibility has always been at the heart of Wideview Innovation. Through Jeeviyam Tuition Centre, we provide free educational support to students from various backgrounds. More than 100 students have benefited, many now pursuing higher education and successful careers.

Co-Proprietor Mr. Dinesh Kumar actively contributes to orphanages, old-age homes, ashrams, charitable institutions, and community welfare initiatives — believing that true success lies in uplifting society and supporting those in need.

  • Free academic tutoring for 100+ students from various backgrounds
  • Career guidance, personality development & skill training programs
  • Part-time job opportunities and event management training for college students
  • Financial assistance and essential supplies for orphanages and old-age homes
  • Educational support initiatives and community welfare programs
