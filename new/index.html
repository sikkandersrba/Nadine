Landmark Events

OUR GREATEST ACHIEVEMENTS

Defining moments that built Wideview Innovation's legacy across Tamil Nadu and South India.

🎬
South Indian Short Film Festival & koneX Awards

5 consecutive years of South India's premier short film awards — recognizing hundreds of aspiring filmmakers with a professional platform comparable to major ceremonies. Organized in collaboration with RTV, RCN TV, and Bava TV.

🏠
Legends of Dance — Texvalley Erode

One of South India's largest dance competitions with 8,000+ participants conducted at Texvalley, Erode. The massive response established Wideview as the go-to partner for large-scale cultural productions in Tamil Nadu.

🏢
Caterpillar MOU Function — SIPCOT Erode

Flawless execution of a major corporate event for Caterpillar at SIPCOT, Erode — appreciated for its world-class professional planning, precision management, and operational excellence that set a new benchmark.

🏆
Talents of Tamil Nadu 2023

A grand talent and fashion platform in 2023 giving aspiring artists, models, performers, and young talents a professional stage. A landmark success that strengthened Wideview's position in Tamil Nadu's entertainment ecosystem.
