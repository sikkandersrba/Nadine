What Is an FCA?

Portfolio Visibility at Engineering Grade

A Facility Condition Assessment is a systematic, multi-discipline evaluation of your infrastructure portfolio — producing standardized condition scores, asset inventory, and lifecycle intelligence that power capital planning decisions.

  • Structural, mechanical, electrical, and life-safety systems evaluated
  • Standardized FCI benchmarking across all asset classes
  • Remaining useful life assessment for every major system
  • Defensible documentation for governance and funding review
  • Integration-ready data for capital planning models

What the FCA Program Produces

Four foundational outputs that power every downstream capital decision.

01

FCI Benchmarking

Standardized Facility Condition Index scores for every building and system — enabling true cross-facility comparison and trend tracking.

02

Asset Inventory

Complete structured inventory of all building systems with classification, condition ratings, installation dates, and estimated replacement costs.

03

Lifecycle Documentation

Remaining useful life projections and replacement timelines for every major system — the foundation of 10–20 year capital forecasting.

04

Governance-Ready Reports

Audit-ready documentation structured for treasury submissions, board review, and public accountability requirements.
