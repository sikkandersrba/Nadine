Capital Intelligence

From Assessment Data to Multi-Year Roadmaps

Nadine transforms FCA data into defensible long-term capital forecasting — producing models that withstand governance scrutiny and align with institutional funding cycles.

  • 10–20 year capital forecasting aligned with funding cycles

  • Board-ready reporting packages and treasury submissions

Methodology

Three Pillars of Capital Modeling

Our capital planning methodology integrates lifecycle intelligence, risk weighting, and scenario simulation into a unified defensible framework.

01

Lifecycle Intelligence

Remaining useful life data from FCA feeds directly into replacement cost projections, creating a time-phased capital need profile across the full portfolio.

02

Risk-Weighted Prioritization

Capital requirements are weighted by safety exposure, regulatory compliance risk, and operational criticality — not simply by condition score rankings.

03

Scenario Modeling

Multiple funding scenarios modeled simultaneously — baseline, accelerated reinvestment, and deferral impact — so leadership can make informed choices before committing capital.
