Why Risk Matters in Capital Planning

Condition alone does not determine urgency.

Two assets with similar condition scores may present very different levels of exposure. Without risk integration, capital planning can overlook critical vulnerabilities. Risk-informed prioritization ensures resources are allocated where exposure is greatest.

Life-Safety Exposure

Risk of harm to occupants, responders, or the public.

Regulatory Risk

Compliance, code, and audit-related exposure.

Operational Disruption

Service continuity and mission-critical impact.

Financial Consequence

Capital and operational cost of system failure.

Public Accountability

Reputational and governance exposure to the public.
