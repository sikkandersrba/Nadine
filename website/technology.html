Platform Architecture

Built on Engineering Discipline, Layer by Layer

Each layer of the Nadine platform serves a specific role in the infrastructure intelligence lifecycle — from raw data capture at the asset level all the way to defensible, auditable outputs in the boardroom.

Field Data Capture Layer

Standardized digital forms, geo-tagged photographs, and condition coding feed directly into the central asset registry — no spreadsheet handoffs, no transcription error.

L1 · Capture

Normalization & Validation Layer

Raw inputs are validated against asset hierarchy rules, FCI scoring standards, and engineering taxonomies — producing one consistent, defensible portfolio record.

L2 · Process

Modeling & Simulation Layer

Lifecycle projections, risk weighting, and parallel scenario simulation run on top of the validated dataset — enabling 10–20 year capital strategy modeling.

L3 · Model

Visualization & Reporting Layer

Live dashboards, executive views, and auto-generated board packages render directly from the model layer — every output traceable back to source assessment data.

L4 · Deliver
