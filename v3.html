Est. 2016 — Erode, Tamil Nadu

Creating Experiences Since 2016

Wideview Innovation was established in Erode in 2016. Founded by Mrs. Vijayalakshmi Krishnamoorthy, with Mr. Dinesh Kumar as the driving force, we began with a prestigious collaboration with RTV and Bharani Silks — organizing a large-scale Drawing Competition that received tremendous public appreciation.

We then partnered with RCN TV and Bava TV for cultural programs, talent shows, and public engagement events — building our reputation as a reliable and creative event management partner across Tamil Nadu.

🎉 500+ Events 🎬 Film Festivals 🏫 Jeeviyam 🏢 Corporate 🍒 Adorez 🤝 CSR

Milestones that shaped Wideview Innovation's identity across South India.

🎬

South Indian Short Film Festival & koneX Awards

Organized for 5 consecutive years, this festival provided hundreds of aspiring filmmakers with professional recognition — supported by RTV, RCN TV, and Bava TV.
